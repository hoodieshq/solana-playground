# Tutorial Feed Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans
> (inline) to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`)
> syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Opening a tutorial with a definition shows its conversation in the
frame's chat column: the intro card with the finished program in the
workspace, then the steps as one feed of dividers, say and quote turns, wrote
cards, act and result cards and their records, with the step bar above it
and the back pill over its foot. Learners can jump back to an earlier step
and return without losing their place.

**Architecture:** A new widget, `widgets/tutorial-conversation` (design D3),
renders `ConversationView` from the engine (task 3.1) and calls
`PgConversation`'s methods. It computes no lesson state of its own. The only
state it keeps is about the screen: which step the feed is scrolled into,
whether the live card is on screen, and the chat's width class. Every card
is a `Base<Name>` that takes everything through props, and one connected
`TutorialConversation` subscribes to the store and wires the explorer.
`views/flow/Flow.tsx` passes it to the frame's `body` slot (task 2.1) in
place of `AssistantConversation`, along with Start over and the passed count.
Opening a definition tutorial creates its workspace from `intro.files`, so
the engine has a save to load, and its cursor starts on the intro.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, Tailwind 4, `shared/ui` (`button`, `kbd`, `tag`,
`spinner`, `collapsible`, `dropdown-menu`, `tooltip`, `input`), lucide icons,
vitest in-source blocks and Testing Library, one Playwright spec.

**Spec:** `openspec/changes/tutorial-flow-v2/specs/tutorial-conversation/spec.md`:
"A tutorial opens on its intro card", "Only the latest card acts", "Each
step opens with a divider and the step bar jumps to it", "The wrote card and
the editor show what the assistant wrote", and the act card's shell from "Act
cards run one thing and show its stages". Design D2 and D3.

## Sources, and where they differ

The prototype (`almaz/mobile-tutorial-prototype`,
`design-system/src/prototype/tutorial-v2`, `ui/Conversation.tsx`,
`ui/StepBar.tsx`, `ui/turns/*`, SPEC.md sections 6, 12 and 13) is the source
for the cards' classes, rhythm and motion. The spec delta is the source for
behaviour. Where the two differ:

- **Narrow step bar:** SPEC.md 6.3 hides the labels under 380 px and keeps
  tooltips; the delta and `ui/StepBar.tsx` turn the bar into one menu button.
  The menu is taken, at the delta's 24rem of chat width.
- **Intro card:** the prototype's "Ask me anything first" focuses the
  composer, which is task 3.6's. It ships with 3.6; this ticket's card has
  "Start step 1" only.
- **Placeholders and chips:** the composer's, task 3.6.
- **Stat pills on the win:** none (delta, "Each step ends on a win card").

Decided here, no source settles it:

- **The workspace on open.** A definition tutorial with no workspace gets
  one when it opens (`PgTutorial.start` with `intro.files`, the default file
  the first step's `file`). The About page never shows for it. Start over
  already returns to the intro inside the workspace, so the intro has one
  home either way (Natalia, 2 Nov). The cost is that a tutorial only opened
  shows in the project list as "0 of 4". A workspace started under the v1
  About page loads a fresh save on the intro, and the intro's Start writes
  step 1's start over its files, as Start over would.
- **Turns built by other tickets.** The question card (3.5) and the win and
  finish cards (3.4) are their tickets'. Until each lands, the feed draws a
  plain stand-in that still moves the lesson on, so Hello Anchor can be
  walked by hand: the question with its three options as buttons
  (`pick`), Hint and Skip; the win's headline and Next; the finish's
  headline. Each carries `TODO(tutorial-flow-v2 3.4)` or `3.5`, and HOO-2352
  replaces the win and finish ones right after this ticket (Natalia,
  2 Nov). `you` pills and `hint` turns are small and built here.
- **The ports.** `init` gets `realWorkspace`, `unwiredRunner` with
  `TODO(tutorial-flow-v2 4.1)` (the run tickets replace it), and 3.2's judge
  if PR 223 has merged by then, else `passingJudge` with
  `TODO(tutorial-flow-v2 3.2)`. With the unwired runner every act fails with
  its "not wired" result, which is the result card's failed shell.
- **Start words.** "start", "begin" and "go" are typed in the composer,
  which task 3.6 builds; 3.6's routing calls `PgConversation.start`. This
  ticket gives the intro Enter when nothing else holds focus (SPEC.md
  6.5.1).
- **The preview is read only** (delta, "A tutorial opens on its intro
  card"). Locking the editor is the workspace's (task 2.2); this ticket
  leaves `TODO(tutorial-flow-v2 2.2)` where the intro is drawn.
- **Lighting the lines** after a jump or a wrote card is the editor's
  `pg-arrive` (task 2.2). Here a wrote card opens the tab and scrolls the
  first line to a third of the editor's height; the step bar opens the
  step's `file` at the top.

## Global Constraints

- The widget imports `@/features/tutorial-conversation`,
  `@/entities/tutorial` and `@/shared`, plus the legacy roots (`utils`) in
  the connected component alone. Nothing imports `views/flow/lessons`.
- The widget never builds a turn: every line it draws comes from `view()`'s
  `Turn` or the definition, so a reload draws the same feed.
- Only the latest card acts (delta). A record has no primary button; its
  navigation links ("Show in lib.rs") stay live.
- A turn with `whole: true` renders without `animate-rise` or typing, and
  `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the rise for every turn and makes the jump
  instant.
- The feed is `role="log"` with `aria-live="polite"`; the step bar is
  `nav aria-label="Tutorial steps"`.
- Ids, `aria-label`s and test ids follow the delta's strings exactly:
  "Step n of m, <title>, done" and "…, current", "Show lines <a> to <b> in
  <file>", "Back to step n", "Back to the start".
- While the engine has no view yet for a definition tutorial, the column
  shows one spinner, with no skeleton.
- Conventional Commits, scope `client-v2`, ticket `HOO-2351` in the subject.

## Telemetry

The conversation's events share the `tutorial` prefix and its
`tutorial_<noun>_<verb>` names (`tutorial_step_passed`,
`tutorial_question_asked`). The prefix belongs to `entities/tutorial`, so,
as the engine's events do (3.1), the feed's are declared on that slice's
tracker. The widget's own `model/telemetry.ts` re-exports it with the
reason, and no prefix is registered. The two events, which the delta gains
under "The conversation is measured" through `/opsx:update`:

- `tutorial_step_jumped` when a step bar segment, a narrow-menu item or
  ⌥↑/⌥↓ moves the feed to a divider, with `tutorial`, `step` (1 to 6, the
  target), `from` (the live step) and `via` (`bar`, `menu` or `key`).
- `tutorial_step_back` when the back pill takes the learner back to the live
  card, with `tutorial` and `step` (the live step, or 0 before Start).

Logs go under `tutorial-conversation:feed`.
