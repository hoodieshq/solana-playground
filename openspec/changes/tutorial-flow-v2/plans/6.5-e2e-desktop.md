# 6.5 e2e for the flow, desktop (HOO-2364)

Goal: Playwright walks Hello Anchor from the intro card to the finish card on
desktop in CI's e2e job, with no network, so a regression in the
conversation, the runs or their wiring fails the PR that brings it.
The phone half of 6.5 is HOO-2510.

## Approach

The specs drive the real app and the real runners. What CI lacks is the
network, so the test answers it:

- **Build server** (`e2e/fake-build-server.ts`): `POST **/build` fails with
  the real E0412 log (`PLANNED` in `model/build-logs.fixture.ts`) while
  `lib.rs` has no `struct Hello`, and passes otherwise with a stable uuid and
  an Anchor 0.29 IDL derived from `lib.rs` (`hello`, plus `name: string` when
  the signature takes it). `GET **/deploy/<uuid>` serves a few KB of bytes.
- **Devnet** (`e2e/fake-devnet.ts`): a stateful JSON-RPC fake on
  `https://api.devnet.solana.com` plus `routeWebSocket` on its `wss://`. It
  decodes each sent transaction with `@solana/web3.js` in the test process and
  applies what the tutorial sends: `SystemProgram.createAccount`, the
  upgradeable loader's initialize buffer, write, deploy, upgrade and extend,
  and an Anchor call, whose logs it writes from the decoded instruction
  (`Hello, World!`, or `Hello, <name>!`). Slots and blockhashes advance per
  call; `signatureSubscribe` is acked and its status is already confirmed.
  `requestAirdrop` credits the wallet, or answers a JSON-RPC error when the
  test refuses it. Anything else answers an empty result.
- **Copy from the definition**: the specs read questions, options and the
  win headlines from `src/tutorials/hello-anchor/tutorial.json`, so tuning the
  content (HOO-2361) does not break them. Only the cards' own UI strings
  ("Build failed", "Not enough devnet SOL", "Upgraded") are literal.

## Specs (`e2e/tutorial-hello-anchor.e2e.spec.ts`)

1. **Intro to finish**, at 1440x900: Start, q1 right, build fails on the
   planned E0412 ("E0412 · line 9"), "I'll fix it myself" and the fix
   question, build passes, win and Next; q2, deploy from a fresh wallet
   ("Create wallet", funding, "4 of 4", Explorer links with `cluster=devnet`);
   step 3's `client.ts` is created, **reload** reopens on the same question
   with no intro, q3, run reads "Confirmed" with "Program log: Hello, World!";
   q4, build and deploy reads "Upgraded" with step 2's program id, the call
   with the name "Alex" wins with no Next and the finish card's receipt log
   reads "Program log: Hello, Alex!".
2. **A wrong pick, then the right one**: q1 option 1 shows "Not quite." and the
   definition's why; the record ends "· 2nd try".
3. **The planned E0412 with Explain**: Explain sends the build log to the
   assistant (stubbed `/api/agent`), then the planned explanation quoting line
   9 and the "Fix it" question follow.
4. **The faucet at its limit**: the airdrop is refused, the red "Not enough
   devnet SOL" card shows with "Open faucet", and Try again under it deploys
   once the faucet answers.
5. **Step 3 passed from the Call panel**: `test.fixme` until HOO-2358 (#357)
   is in `scope/tutorial-flow`; written against its scenario.
